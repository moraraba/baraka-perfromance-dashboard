# CFO note: Baraka performance 2023–2025

**To:** CFO · **From:** Finance analytics · **Re:** What drives profit, and what to change in the 2027 budget

Sales grew 13.9% in two years, from KES 94.2M (2023) to KES 107.3M (2025), but profit grew only 5.7%, from KES 25.3M to KES 26.8M. Margin slipped every year: 26.9%, 25.8%, 25.0%. Three findings explain most of it.

## Findings

1. **Costs are rising while prices stand still.** Average unit cost rose about 2% a year on every product (cooking oil: KES 232 to 241 per unit) while the realised price stayed flat (KES 339 both years). This alone explains most of the 1.9-point margin drop.
   *Chart: Page 1, monthly sales and profit trend vs last year; Page 2, margin by product.*

2. **Discounts cost KES 15.7M over three years, half of it to Distributors.** The High band (15% off) earns a 13.8% margin against 31.0% at no discount. Distributors receive 49.5% of all discount shillings, put 42.5% of their gross sales through the High band, and earn just 11.2% margin there.
   *Chart: Page 2, margin by discount band and segment matrix.*

3. **Kigali broke in July 2024 and has not recovered.** Kigali sales fell 25% year on year in July 2024 and stayed about 20% down for twelve months. 2025 sales were KES 3.8M against KES 4.6M in 2023, about 27% below its share of plan. Every other market grew 14–17%. The business as a whole beat plan in 2023 (+2.1%), missed it in all twelve months of 2024 (−2.1%), and was back on plan from September 2025.
   *Chart: Page 3, actual vs target by month and market table.*

## Recommendations

1. **Give Small shops more budget, and back Mango Juice.** Small shops earn the highest margin (32.3%), grew fastest (+16.0% since 2023), take the least discount (3.2%) and are already the second-largest profit pool (KES 16.9M). Mango Juice grew 140% since 2023 at a 35.3% margin, the best of any product. Protect Supermarkets, still the largest profit pool at KES 35.6M.
   *Chart: Page 2, profit and margin by segment and by product.*

2. **Tighten the discount policy for Distributors.** Make the High band an exception that needs finance approval, with volume commitments in return. Moving half of Distributors' High-band gross sales (about KES 6M a year) to the Medium band (7%) would keep roughly KES 0.5M a year in profit, if volumes hold. Track Discount % by segment monthly.
   *Chart: Page 2, discount band matrix.*

3. **Reprice for cost, and fix Kigali before it rejoins the plan.** List prices have not moved in three years while costs climbed. A 2% price rise on 2025 volumes is worth about KES 2.1M after discounts, about 8% of 2025 profit, if demand holds. Separately, find out what changed in Kigali in July 2024 (a lost distributor, a price or border issue) and set market-level targets for 2027 so a gap like this shows up in the first month, not the twelfth.
   *Chart: Page 3, Kigali actual vs allocated target.*
